## Visual language (observed)

- **Style:** hand-painted / illustrated, textured, hand-lettered all-caps type.
- **Palette (eyeballed, not sampled):** black and charcoal base; olive / lime green accents
  (the "IMPACT > INCOME" line, checkmarks, underlines); warm wood browns; cream paper; white text.
- **Imagery:** outdoors — waterfall, rainforest, mountains. Black cowboy hat, wraparound
  sunglasses, black tee. Rugged, self-made, outdoor-working.
- **Logo cue:** "ST" monogram with mountain peaks above it.

## Recurring themes (count of appearances on the board)

- **Freedom** — 8 (headline, chalkboard, laptop, plaque, mug, book, sticky note, keychain)
- **Family / Provider** — 3 · **Legacy** — 2 · **Discipline** — 2 · **Faith** — 1
- **Deals / Wealth / Real Estate / Finance** — present throughout

## Status (settled 2026-10-03)

- **"ST" is the owner's PERSONAL brand, not S&S Remodels LLC.** The board is a reference for
  his personal look, tone and values. It is **not** the S&S logo or the S&S brand.
- **This repo's only job is the S&S Remodels LLC website.** Use the board for feel only
  (rugged, outdoor, black + green, hand-lettered, "built different"); do not put the ST
  monogram or the wealth / deals / real-estate messaging on the S&S site.
